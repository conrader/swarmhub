## Goal
No system bar and no app name inside the main window. The traffic lights stay (system-placed) over a short sidebar row that holds the three icon
buttons; the chat, group, Details and Members headers (54 pt) become the window's top edge and its draggable strips, as in Grok Bot. `NSWindow.title`
stays "Torvli", hidden and never blanked (Window menu, VoiceOver, `UISmoke.mainWindow()`). Settings and Computer windows keep default chrome.
