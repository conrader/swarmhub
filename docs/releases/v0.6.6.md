SwarmHub 0.6.6 (build 31). Notarized. Routine suggestions now create the routine in the box's time zone and deliver it locally, so it runs at the time you were offered, across daylight saving time changes too (Hermes runs cron in UTC on our boxes; the app moves a suggested routine when the clocks change), and accepting a suggestion no longer leads to a duplicate. Off-app alerts (ntfy/email while SwarmHub is closed) are switched off for now: the Settings section is hidden, nothing about alerts is sent to or installed on a box, and a box that had them on stops sending once swarmhub-messages 1.4.14 reaches it. They come back in a later release. Ships without the embedded baseline; signed baseline (P1b) follows in a later release.
