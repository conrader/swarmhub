**SwarmHub 0.5.6**

- **Connect apps window**: the **Connect apps** button at the bottom left of the sidebar opens a window with app logos, a search field and a short explanation of Composio, the service that keeps your app sign-ins and runs app actions when your bots ask. Each connected account shows which bots use it, with a switch per bot.
- **Several accounts of any app**: connect two Gmails, two GitHubs or more (up to 10 per app per bot). The bot names the account it uses, and your approval card shows it too.
- **Accounts named after who they are**: SwarmHub reads each account's email address or username once and uses it as the account's name, instead of a random id. You can rename any account.
- **A new app is on for every bot**: an app you connect from the Connect apps window or Settings is switched on for all your bots; turn it off per bot. A connect card in a chat still turns it on only for that bot.
- **Clearer app errors**: when an app action fails, the chat shows the reason Composio gives, and the bot gets the failure back instead of stopping silently.
- **Orientation for new bots**: a bot created by your manager (or a new bot from a Grok import) introduces itself, starts a read-only first look at its area, tells its coordinator, and says when it will report. Settings → Advanced → Orientation has the coordinator and quiet hours. Existing bots are not affected.
