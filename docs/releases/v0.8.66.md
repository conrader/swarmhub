# SwarmHub 0.8.66

- Get an alert on your device when a watched pull request fails CI, even after closing the workspace. On iPhone, add your workspace to the Home Screen from Safari and open it there. Choose **Activity → CI alerts on this device** to turn alerts on.
- Tap a CI notification to open **Needs attention**. The lock screen keeps repository names, check details and conversation content private. Update the workspace server to 0.2.37 to enable delivery.
