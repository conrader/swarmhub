# SwarmHub 0.8.19

- After restarting SwarmHub, open a chat on a password-protected SSH server and click **Sign In…** to reconnect. Your draft stays in the chat while you sign in. SwarmHub remembers the password until you quit.
- Settings → What’s New lists publicly released versions. Changes from the skipped 0.8.17 candidate are listed under 0.8.18.
