# SwarmHub 0.8.18

- Reopen a long chat at its saved reading position or jump to its latest reply. Switching between bots keeps the draft, quoted message and unread status with the right conversation. Images and videos you already opened are available offline.
- Steer Codex while it prepares a reply. Click the model in the chat header to see which model is answering and select the next one. Change the bot’s model and its coding helper’s model separately. The next message carries your conversation across a provider change.
- Keep chatting while a helper works. Read its result, stop it or promote it to a bot. Open Activity to review commands and see which background jobs still need attention.
- Open **Manage AIs on this Mac** from a bot’s model settings to scan for newly installed CLIs, including Gemini and Antigravity. The setup scan avoids Codex’s interactive updater.
- Use **Set up on server** in the wizard or Settings to install the workspace over SSH. Custom SSH ports are supported. Setup shows the download and startup stages, runs on Debian 12 x86_64 and can repair an incompatible workspace.
- Press ⌘K to find shared Pages by title and open them from chat. The Media tab shows Pages alongside chat attachments.
- Reopen Details from the chat header. Review an available app update even with the sidebar hidden. On iPhone, open pending approvals with **Review** in the chat header.
