# SwarmHub 0.8.20

- The Mac app now uses the Interactive preview’s dark and light surfaces, blue default accent and message colors. You can still choose your own accent in Settings.
- Expand a bot in the sidebar to see its helper sessions, including helpers it started in group chats. Select a session to inspect its status, task and results. The sidebar remembers each bot’s disclosure setting.
- Move a bot between This Mac and an SSH server while it is answering a direct chat. SwarmHub checks that the source stopped, carries over the request and available progress, and queues a continuation. Its routines move with their schedules and pause settings. Chats waiting for approvals, group runs, active goals and detached workers stay on the source.
