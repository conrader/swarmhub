**SwarmHub 0.5.4**

- **Chats never block each other**: message other bots while one is thinking. Up to 6 replies run at once (4 per box); a message over the limit waits with "Waiting: 6 replies are already running." **Stop** stops only its own chat, and updating SwarmHub's instructions in your manager's chat no longer freezes the others.
- **Connected apps with no sign-in**: pick an app under Settings → Connected apps and click **Connect**. SwarmHub sets up your app connections on the first one; the only page you see is that app's own permission page. Your own Composio key is still possible under Advanced. Until SwarmHub's server finishes its setup, the pane says "App connections aren't available yet."
