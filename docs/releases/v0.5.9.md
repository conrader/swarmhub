**SwarmHub 0.5.9**

- **App requests are never silently ignored**: when a bot sends an app request with an old marker or a stray `}`, SwarmHub now corrects it and the bot retries; a tool the bot made up goes back to it as "unknown tool, search first". If a request still can't run, the chat says "This app request was not run".
- **Every account named by who it is**: Google Calendar, Drive, Slack and Notion accounts now show their email or user name, like Gmail and GitHub. Unnamed accounts are retried, and a name you set yourself always wins.
- **Live voice says why it failed**: the voice window keeps the error and names the step: microphone off (with a button to the setting), ChatGPT refusing live voice (with its reason), no answer, a dropped connection or the audio device. **Copy details** helps report it.
- **Bots know their heartbeat**: each bot is told which routine is its heartbeat and its schedule, and SwarmHub's manager skill (1.3.0) explains how to change it. A schedule changed on the box shows as "changed on the box", with **Use the box's schedule** or **Put mine back**.
