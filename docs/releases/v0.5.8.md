**SwarmHub 0.5.8**

- **Reading runs on its own**: app actions that only read, such as checking email, getting a profile, listing or searching, no longer ask for your approval; they show as one folded line in the chat. Anything that sends, writes, edits, creates, deletes, follows or stars still asks, as do raw queries (like GitHub GraphQL), tools that fetch web addresses, and reads that carry a web address, someone else's email address or very long text. Anything SwarmHub can't classify asks.
- **App failures don't stop the work**: when an app action fails or its answer can't be read, the bot is told and continues, a `/goal` keeps going, and the chat says what failed. Failures are logged on your Mac without their contents.
