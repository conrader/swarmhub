# SwarmHub 0.8.111

- Selecting a helper opens its conversation. Read its assignment and reply, expand its steps, or send a follow-up through the parent while its session is available. Session details and the terminal remain accessible. Protected assignments are labeled as unavailable.
- A goal result that arrives from the workspace after a restart is matched to the original iteration. Chat shows the answer and its steps without an obsolete Incomplete label or the internal JSON report.
- Settings → General can automatically hide finished helpers. In manual mode, right-click a finished helper and choose Remove from sidebar. Restore manually removed entries in General. Active descendants and session history are preserved.
