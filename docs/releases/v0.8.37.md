# SwarmHub 0.8.37

- More of your team fits in the sidebar. The main bot’s star appears beside its name, and the AI for a bot with a reported runtime appears at the right edge of its name line. Smaller avatars and tighter spacing leave more room for names, message previews and indented helpers.
- A running bot shows the AI reported for its current session, even before you open its chat or when a different AI is selected for the next turn. Idle bots show the AI selected for their next session as soon as the workspace reports it.
