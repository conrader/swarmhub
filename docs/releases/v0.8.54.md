# SwarmHub 0.8.54

- Open a working Claude helper under its bot in the sidebar or in the terminal panel and choose **Ask Claude to stop**. Other helpers can keep working. The app shows **Stop requested** until Claude reports what happened.
- A stopped helper keeps its task and output. When Claude resumes that helper, the next run appears in the same session.
- The control is available for Claude sessions launched with workspace 0.2.32 or later. Existing conversations keep their history.
