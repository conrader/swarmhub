# SwarmHub 0.8.86

- Choose **/goal** in a sample bot’s composer to track an objective while you try its walkthrough. Its progress, step limit and spending limit stay with that bot when you switch chats or reopen the app. You can stop tracking at any time.
- Choose **+ Screenshot** in a sample chat to attach the example dialog, edit the question, and see the measured contrast of its secondary button. The paperclip still accepts your own images.
- The sample context meter in the chat header and Details now advances as you send messages.
